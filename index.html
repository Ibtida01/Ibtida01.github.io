PROJECT / 01Innovation Fair 2026

Project DRISHTI — AI-powered diabetic retinopathy screening

An EfficientNet-B6 deep-learning pipeline for automated diabetic retinopathy detection and severity grading, designed with rural screening settings in mind. The workflow combines Circular Fundus Isolation, Ben Graham Gaussian subtraction, and CLAHE preprocessing. Continuous regression predictions are optimized with Nelder–Mead thresholding, reaching a reported Quadratic Weighted Kappa (QWK) of 0.9102. Presented at Innovation Fair 2026, including a presentation to Mr. Mahdi Amin, Advisor to the Prime Minister of Bangladesh.

PROJECT / 022nd Runner-Up

ONE — Unified citizen-service platform

An AI-powered intake platform designed to reduce counter-to-counter referrals in Bangladeshi banks and government offices. People can describe their needs in Bengali, English, or Banglish; the platform issues a token, pre-fills forms, and prepares an officer briefing. The system covers 84 services across 10 sectors, with a reported sub-three-second AI response using Groq and a three-tier fallback.

PROJECT / 03Live

Shobarkhamar — AI fish & poultry disease detection

“Everyone's Farm” is an AI diagnostic platform for Bangladeshi fish and poultry farmers. It serves predictions from EfficientNet-B4 across 8 fish classes and EfficientNetV2 across 5 poultry classes, alongside treatment recommendations, farm management, and diagnosis history.
